Onam is the vibrant, 10-day harvest festival of Kerala, celebrating the legendary homecoming of the benevolent Asura King Mahabali and marking the Malayalam New Year. Observed by all Malayalis regardless of religion, the festival transforms the state with stunning Pookkalam (floral carpets) at doorsteps, majestic snake boat races (Vallam Kali) on the backwaters, and high-energy cultural dances like the Pulikali tiger dance. The festivities reach their spectacular peak on the final day, Thiruvonam, when families gather to enjoy the Onam Sadya, an unforgettable, multi-course vegetarian feast served traditionally on a banana leaf.The undisputed crown jewel of the celebration is the Onam Sadya, a magnificent, multi-course vegetarian feast featuring up to 30 distinct dishes served traditionally on a fresh banana leaf, symbolizing the country's rich agricultural abundance and deep-rooted spirit of togetherness.
