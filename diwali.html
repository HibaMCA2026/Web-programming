Diwali, also known as Deepavali, is the prominent Hindu festival of lights that symbolises the spiritual victory of light over darkness, good over evil, and knowledge over ignorance. Derived from the Sanskrit word dīpāvalī, meaning "row of lights", it is India's most significant and widely celebrated holiday. Observed by over a billion people globally, the festival spans five days during the Hindu lunisolar months of Ashvin and Kartika (typically falling between mid-October and mid-November). While primarily a Hindu festival, distinct variations of Diwali are also celebrated in Jainism, Sikhism, and by some Buddhists.
