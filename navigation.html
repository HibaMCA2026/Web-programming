About Us

MEA Engineering College is accredited by NAAC. As per the data released by APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU) on 15.06.2026, the college has secured the first position in Malappuram district.

  • Computer Science & Engineering (CSE)
  • Artificial Intelligence & Data Science
  • Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE)
  • Electrical & Electronics Engineering (EEE)
  • Mechanical Engineering
  • Civil Engineering

Vision and Mission

Our Vision: To provide top class education to the community by achieving excellence in engineering education.

Our Mission: To provide the best faculty, excellent infrastructure, and commendable facilities for an excellent academic ambiance and to encourage research.
