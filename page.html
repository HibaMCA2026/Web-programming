About Us


Mea engineering college is accredited by NAAC. As per the data released by APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU) on 15.06.2026, the college has secured the first position in Malappuram district. It also holds the 13th position among all self-financing engineering colleges in Kerala and the 30th position among all listed engineering colleges in Kerala.

Courses offered

MEA Engineering College (MEAEC) in Malappuram, Kerala, offers a variety of undergraduate (B.Tech), postgraduate (M.Tech, BCA, MCA), and doctoral (Ph.D.) programmes. The institution is affiliated with the APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU) and approved by the AICTE.>
